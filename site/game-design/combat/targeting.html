Targeting and assessment ​

Design status: Accepted direction and experimental resolver policy. Implementation: Stage 2A natural resolution integrated; aimed resolver remains pure and no player intent is stored.

A player may prefer a body region while automatic combat continues. The server still decides whether a swing occurs, whether it hits and where it lands. Selection does not queue an extra attack, pause combat, reset recovery or guarantee a region.

Accepted experimental first resolver ​

The approved policy for later hit integration gives valid aim a 50% placeholder chance to choose the intended region. Failure samples natural coverage excluding that entire intended region. Unrestricted fallback would inflate success to p + (1-p) × naturalWeight, so it is not the specified prototype. Natural/no-intent uses no aim roll. Aimed arm contact chooses Hands:Arms in a 1:2 ratio.

Revalidate the effective defender after redirection. Missing armor does not reroll location. Intent can survive a miss or brief loss of melee range, but Combatant/session/map/lifecycle changes invalidate context. A stale response must not clear a newer valid selection.

Assessment is planned to use existing gumps and explicit snapshots in Slice 0. Stage 2A now provides the approved qualitative Anatomy path; native hotkeys remain separate work. Gap: no selection service, revision handling or live aiming has been implemented. Resolver checks do not establish live aiming or gameplay validation.

Proposed targeting factors and knowledge ​

Batch 03 retains aiming as intent, never a guaranteed permanent region lock. Opportunities, position, attack execution, weapon, character skill, defender movement/defense, available information and stamina/fatigue are candidate influences. Their final accuracy/cost relationships remain Unresolved; they do not replace the accepted experimental resolver above.

Anatomy can support informed target choice through body-condition knowledge. Accepted disclosure remains the boundary: the proposal grants no new hidden numerical information. Tactics and Arms Lore supply distinct opportunity/equipment roles. Stamina and reactive input records the unresolved manual-input conflict without changing scheduled attacks.
