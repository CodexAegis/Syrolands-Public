Unresolved decisions ​

AreaQuestions retained for later design and evaluation
Location and intentFinal natural limb distribution; aimed accuracy, cost and contributing factors. Existing experimental weights and 50% aimed policy remain intact, not final balance.
Stamina and tempoAttack costs, fatigue representation, regeneration, recovery timing, missed/canceled attempts and repeated-action pressure.
Stats and martial skillsBounded DEX and STR contributions; Tactics role beyond damage scaling; avoid a universal mandatory weapon/Tactics/Anatomy/Healing bundle.
Injuries and medicineBleeding thresholds, wound types, Healing/stabilization, local recovery and persistence beyond the session fixture.

Combat balancePvP time-to-kill targets and PvM scaling; shared rules without imposing human anatomy on every creature.
Creatures and rangeNPC eligibility, large-creature profiles, nonhuman anatomy and ranged targeted shots.
Input and animationWhether manual attacks are optional or replace auto; weapon timing, animation capabilities and client limitations.
Network and accessReactive-input networking, latency fairness, remapping/accessibility, anti-macro/automation implications and unsupported-client behavior.

Canonical ownership and reconciliation ​

The body model, trauma policy, targeting resolver and shield rule are preserved. Batch 03 expands the proposal around them; it does not silently supersede accepted experiments. Weapon identity, armor interaction and stamina/input centralize the new directions.

Quality definitions, stats, skills and knowledge, profession maintenance, loss economics and Magic retain their own authority. Proposed high-end equipment cannot change the prototype trauma formula or accepted replaceability by implication.
