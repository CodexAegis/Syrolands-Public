Magic overview ​

Design status: Proposed. Numeric examples: tuning targets only. Implementation: this task changes documentation only; no magic-redesign implementation or runtime validation is established here.

Existing Accepted foundations—server authority, global Hits and local integrity, and separate Local Trauma—remain in force. New magic mechanics below remain Proposed unless explicitly identified as an inherited foundation. Named institutions, spells, glyphs and stances are illustrative examples, not established world content.

Identity and principles ​

Redesign the magic ecosystem from first principles while retaining useful UO ideas. Original spells are a library of ideas that may be retained, reworked, renamed, relocated or removed; no legacy spell is automatically guaranteed a place.

ConceptIntended role
MageryGeneral execution capability
Spell knowledgeTechniques the character has permanently learned
Mastery / proficiencyDepth of understanding and specialization
Academies, orders and mastersWhere and how knowledge is acquired
InscriptionMagical engineering, encoding and modification
MeditationInternal regulation, concentration and mental states

The proposal preserves these principles:

  1. Exploration expands magical vocabulary; high Magery does not automatically grant all spells.
  2. Specialization improves reliability and depth, rather than merely damage.
  3. Magic participates in the same localized combat model as melee.
  4. Forbidden knowledge has social, geographical and mechanical consequences.
  5. Inscription remains a major crafting and economic profession.
  6. Meditation contributes active character-building choices beyond mana recovery.
  7. Concentration creates combat pressure and counterplay.
  8. Academies are world institutions, not complete-spellbook shops.
  9. Consumable scrolls provide temporary access without replacing permanent learning.
  10. Modifiers offer horizontal tactical trade-offs rather than mandatory upgrades.
  11. Preserve UO's classless spirit and viable hybrids; do not spend the whole skill cap on separate elemental skills.
  12. Make useful early magic accessible without tedious macroing, while deep mastery requires long-term exploration and experience.

Knowledge and world institutions ​

Permanent learning ​

A properly learned spell becomes permanent character knowledge. A character with Magery 100 might know few spells; another might know many but lack the execution capability to cast difficult ones reliably. Rare knowledge should express the character's history.

Knowledge comes from travel, academies, orders, cults, independent or hidden masters, grimoires, ruins, bosses, questlines, world events and supernatural entities. Formal academies do not monopolize every technique. Learning a base spell need not end exploration: another teacher might offer a rare formula or execution method for it.

Four independent dimensions ​

DimensionMeaningIllustrative candidates, not final taxonomy
DisciplineWhat the mage doesDestruction, Alteration, Conjuration / Invocation, Restoration, Illusion, Abjuration, Divination, Translocation, Necromancy, Voodoo
Affinity / manifestationPhenomenon, medium or expressionFire, Frost / Ice, Lightning, Poison, Shadow, Blood, Arcane, Earth, Wind
TechniqueA learned spellWall of Fire, Incinerate, Napalm, Blink, Flame Ward, Heat Metal, Raise Corpse, Sense Magic
Tradition / institutionWho teaches it, under which philosophy and restrictionsAcademy, order, cult, independent master

A fire specialist need not be a Destruction specialist. Fire can support alteration, protection, conjuration and utility. Do not collapse these dimensions into one generic Fire School containing all fire spells.

Illustration: an Ashen Collegium near a volcano might teach destructive fire, heat manipulation, fire protection and ash conjuration. A military war college might overlap in fire while emphasizing siege, smoke, signals, battlefield control and area denial. Partial overlap gives a specialist reasons to travel between institutions. These names do not select actual map locations or create factions.

Admission, lessons and standing ​

Separate requirements to enter, requirements to learn a particular technique, and conditions to remain in good standing. Candidate gates include tuition, Magery thresholds, reputation, faction standing, quests, a boss defeat, proof of technique, rare objects, moral/criminal alignment, PK-only admission, regional trials and prior membership elsewhere.

Requirements should express identity: a commercial academy charges tuition; a territorial order asks for regional service; a clandestine circle demands complicity; an elite master requests demonstrated technique. Losing favor should normally restrict future teaching, services, ranks or facilities rather than erase already learned knowledge. Partially incompatible affiliations are possible but unresolved.

Magery: execution and progression ​

Attempt floor versus execution Difficulty ​

Each spell should distinguish Minimum Magery, the floor for attempting it, from Difficulty, the challenge of successful execution. Eligibility is not competence. Prefer a nonlinear reliability curve; no equation or percentage is adopted.

Numeric illustrations only: Minimum Magery 50 could permit frequent failures at 50 and improved reliability above it. Minimum Magery 100 could still describe a spell that fails meaningfully at skill 100 when Difficulty is near or beyond the cap. Major resurrection, large-scale teleportation, dangerous summons, battlefield rituals or forbidden magic are examples of spells beyond reliable generalist mastery, not finalized abilities.

The tuning direction is unreliable at the low eligible end, competent through the middle and reliable at high skill, with extreme spells still fallible. Costs and modifiers remain unresolved.

Use-based gain and structured teaching ​

Casting attempts should be able to improve Magery. Relative challenge matters: near-capability spells provide learning opportunities, trivial spells cease to train very high skill, and wildly inappropriate difficulty may also be inefficient. The exact gain curve and whether/how failed attempts contribute are not finalized.

Academies also offer meaningful progression through exercises, examinations, quests, materials, fees and demonstrations. Approximately +5 skill per training block and roughly Magery 55–60 during an initial focused day are illustrative progression/pacing targets, not guarantees, configuration values or locked balance.

Early competence should be accessible; the middle combines teaching and practice; higher skill needs difficult use, specialized teachers, rare training and experience. NPCs should not trivially train a character to 100. This proposal does not change the current skill-cap/progression foundation.

Fizzle versus interruption ​

ConceptMeaningProposed influences
FizzleFailure to execute the magical structure correctlyMagery versus effective Difficulty and relevant modifiers
InterruptionCombat pressure disrupts a cast the mage could otherwise executeDamage, movement, local trauma, concentration loss, state changes or disabling effects

Keep these mechanically distinct. Do not infer reagent, mana, cooldown or recovery costs for either outcome until specified.

Mastery without rigid classes ​

Do not automatically turn disciplines or affinities into eight or ten separate 0–100 skills. Mastery may live outside the classic skill-cap system; its representation and limits remain open. Preserve viable hybrids and meaningful generalists.

The primary proposal is reduced effective Difficulty for advanced relevant spells, with little benefit to basics and greater specialist usefulness for difficult or extreme techniques. A fire specialist could execute Incinerate more reliably than an equally skilled generalist. Mastery is not a flat damage multiplier.

Other candidate benefits include reduced backlash, finer area placement, advanced targeting, better information, special execution modes and field-specific advantages. Novice, Adept, Scholar and Master are possible qualitative tiers, not final ranks.

Progression may consider techniques known, rarity, variety, teachers, institutions visited, quests, practical use and advanced modifiers. Avoid a purchasable spell-count checklist or a fixed wiki shopping route. No count, weight, unlock threshold or incompatibility rule is approved.

Localized magic and injury ​

Magic must engage the same anatomical model as weapons; each spell needs an impact/damage/trauma profile appropriate to its form rather than just a generic HP number. Retain global damage versus Local Trauma, independent regions and server-resolved outcomes.

Illustrative formCandidate interaction, not an implemented rule
ProjectileA specific anatomical location selected by impact profile/targeting
ExplosionTrauma distributed over multiple regions
Ground AoEDisproportionate leg/foot exposure; feet are not newly approved standalone body regions
CurseSpecific limb impairment
ThermalBurns, tissue damage or heated armor
FrostStiffness, mobility impairment, fragility or local freezing
LightningSpasms, temporary control loss or precision penalties
Kinetic / forceBlunt trauma, fractures or knockback
Acid / corrosionArmor wear and tissue damage
Necromantic / supernaturalVitality effects that need not behave like blunt/slash trauma

Do not automatically apply the legacy melee armor adapter, residual ×2 trauma, one-primary-region rule or current HP-loss gate to every magic form. Exposure, armor/resistance, multi-region budgets, repeated area contacts and non-damaging curses need their own explicit contracts. The melee slice retains its existing scope; this proposal does not implement propagation or alter its tests.

Normal and Focused Cast ​

Proposed: Normal Cast uses the spell's natural anatomical profile. Focused Cast attempts a known limb, requires sufficient information/Anatomy, and adds meaningful execution Difficulty, time, resources or another cost. Selection remains intent, not a guaranteed hit/location. Exact interaction with targeting is unresolved; do not copy its experimental 50% melee probability into casting.

Illustration: an informed mage aims Ice Lance at the target's anatomical left leg and accepts greater execution difficulty. Relevant Mastery might offset part of the added difficulty. Flash Freeze could exploit a Wounded leg, Combust Wound damaged tissue, or a targeted curse an already weak arm. These are proposed interactions supporting tactical reading, not a DPS rotation or approved wound multipliers.

Current experimental Anatomy disclosure is not automatically the final targeting prerequisite for magic. Determine required knowledge, freshness and visibility before designing its contract.

Casting components and injured limbs ​

Candidate component tags include Verbal, Gestural, Focused and Ritual; spells need not depend on the same components. Severe head trauma might destabilize concentration/Meditation; arm injury might penalize gestures; chest trauma might affect breath, endurance, long channels or rituals. Tags could interact with silence and restraint.

One-Handed Casting and Silent Casting are examples of rare learned techniques, not automatic generic perks. Exact thresholds, alternatives and component rules are unresolved. These proposed penalties do not supersede Slice 0's limited disability rules or authorize extra functional penalties in the current melee implementation.

Meditation and Concentration ​

Player guide. This section remains the single owner of mental regulation and cast stability; Active Memory owns loadout capacity and reference rules.

Batch 04, Proposed: Meditation can organize and rearrange Active Memory, prepare difficult patterns and recover mental readiness. Higher Meditation may make loadout changes faster/safer and Concentration recovery stronger; no formula, instantaneous in-combat swap or free preparation is granted. Damage, movement, local trauma, blunt/stun effects, repeated pressure, fatigue, item traits and difficult variants are pressure candidates, not newly implemented penalties. Preserve the distinction below between execution fizzle and interruption; Concentration is not automatically another capped skill.

Meditation is mental control and internal magical regulation as well as mana recovery. Skill represents capacity; specific meditative techniques represent knowledge. Advanced techniques may be taught by monasteries, military orders, academies, cults or hidden masters, with precise acquisition/unlock rules still open. States are not ordinary spells and should be commitments with trade-offs, not free buff toggles.

Illustrative statePossible benefitPossible commitment/cost
Trance of StillnessStrong mana recovery, stable concentration, lower DifficultyImmobility/severe movement penalty, reduced awareness, exit delay
Battle MeditationModerate recovery under combat pressure, interruption resistanceStamina penalty, reduced mobility or another trade-off
Empty MindResistance to Illusion, Fear, Charm or mental VoodooWeaker offensive Divination or summon maintenance
Flow StateMore stable coherent discipline/affinity useSwitching fields breaks the state
Deep ChannelAccess to extremely difficult attemptsHigher mana use and vulnerability
Ember TranceFire-specialist stability and lower effective DifficultyOther affinities may become harder

Ember Trance illustrates interaction between Fire Mastery and strong Meditation, not a finalized stance or requirement.

Concentration under pressure ​

Concentration is a proposed additional gameplay layer, not necessarily another visible 0–100 skill. It might be a dynamic resource, internal state or semi-visible meter; representation and UI are unresolved.

Candidate influences include hits, movement, local trauma, target changes, summon maintenance, mental effects, complex casts and interruptions. Meditation may restore, stabilize or preserve it. Advanced casting may require sufficient Mana, Magery and Concentration. Remaining mana alone should not make a pressured mage behave identically to an undisturbed one; melee can disrupt control before depleting health. Recovery, decay, thresholds and interruption formulas are not selected.

Forbidden traditions ​

Necromancy ​

Proposed identity: death, corpses, souls, reanimation, vitality transfer and post-mortem states. Knowledge might be found in crypts, cults, hidden masters, forbidden grimoires, ruins and undead entities, rather than a normal public academy catalogue. Learning may affect institutional/faction relationships. Do not introduce generic “evil points” without a wider world-design decision.

Voodoo: sympathetic magic and personal links ​

Voodoo has a distinct proposed identity: using personal materials/remnants to establish a connection to a particular character. It is not a synonym for Necromancy.

Illustrative loop: kill or discover another player's corpse → obtain a personal remnant (head, blood, hair, bone) through an eligible interaction → perform a ritual at an altar → create a Voodoo Effigy linked to that character → use effects under defined conditions. Anatomy, Forensics, Voodoo knowledge, Carpentry and other crafting skills are possible contributors, not assigned requirements. Ownership, extraction eligibility and altar recipes remain unresolved.

Illustrative effectPossible purpose
Needle of AgonyRemote/semi-remote damage
Withering CurseTemporary stat or regeneration reduction
Limping HexSpecific leg impairment
Silence of the TongueCasting disruption
Marked BloodDirectional tracking
Dream FeverGradual debuff
Binding ThreadInterference with Recall/Gate/Translocation
Shared PainA form of linked incoming damage
Effigy RupturePowerful one-use effect consuming the effigy

Counterplay is required by the proposal: one death must not enable unlimited permanent harassment. Candidate constraints include decaying links/remnant potency, proximity for strongest effects, material consumption, bounded maintained links, Divination detection/identification, purification to sever links and defensive wards. Exact durations, range, costs, caps and victim protections remain unresolved; the examples grant no unrestricted remote-PvP capability.

The intended fantasy—“that enemy possesses a part of me”—connects PvP, corpses, Anatomy/Forensics, housing, Carpentry, ritual crafting, Inscription, Divination and counter-magic. It still needs a coherent anti-grief policy before implementation.

Proposed environmental interaction ​

Living world and materials proposes shared material responses to heat, water, cold, lightning, wind and earth effects. Fireball supplies generic heat/ignition stimuli rather than object-specific burn scripts. Environmental spell effects, weather integration and tactical terrain changes remain Proposed; the candidate tree/grass prototype is not implementation authorization or a final spell list.

Integration boundary and sources ​

Authority: client intent might request a focused cast; ModernUO must resolve eligibility, targets, affected tiles, duration, damage, trauma, status and collision/area effects. Synchronized data would contain only server-approved information; ClassicUO would present effects/cues. No client animation or particle collision may decide gameplay.

Visual ambitions are a separate technical concern; stock UO effects must not define spell design. No effect ID, packet, persistence schema or renderer has been allocated here.

Current implementation and gap: existing documentation records natural localized melee and experimental Anatomy in current slice. It does not establish this magic ecosystem as implemented. Neither the original integration nor Batch 04 establishes a fresh audit of legacy spell code or changes its behavior. See the inheritance evidence boundaries. Permanent learning, institutions, mastery, scroll/Inscription changes, concentration and localized casting need later approved technical designs, bounded implementations and playtests. New magic remains outside the current slice; no accepted ADR is superseded by this proposal.

Profession and item-quality proposals ​

Alchemy and Cooking propose preparations/food that may interact with recovery or Concentration; Tinkering leaves magical construct binding as a future possibility. Item traits and latent resonance need compatibility and counterplay rather than implied spell effects.

The profession reconciliation register keeps global teaching/gain direction separate from Magery's illustrative training blocks and execution Difficulty. No numeric pacing, new Concentration skill, Inscription rule or spellbook-loss exception is accepted by Batch 01.

Batch 02 progression and INT reconciliation ​

Skills & Skillcap centralizes the general Proposed gain/cap/teacher framework, including the 80–90 Very slow band. The +5 blocks and 55–60 first-day examples above remain magic-specific illustrations, not general rates. Reliable routine low-skill work and fallible advanced spell Difficulty need deliberate reconciliation; this addition changes neither the spell model nor its existing text.

INT direction proposes cognition, mental resilience and magical resource capability beyond mana size. Its relationship with Meditation, Concentration, spell knowledge and mental states remains Unresolved; no complete INT system or new skill is implied.

Batch 03 combat pressure boundary ​

Stamina and reactive combat links damage, local trauma, movement, repeated pressure, stun/impact and weapon-trait candidates to the existing Proposed Concentration design. This does not select interruption formulas, create stun mechanics, redefine fizzle, or require a standalone Concentration skill. Magic remains the canonical owner of these distinctions.

Batch 04 provenance and progression ​

Batch 04 (A–X) adds Core Patterns, bounded glyphs, per-pattern Familiarity, Active Memory, INT cognitive workspace, material risk, Inscription and modular visuals. The reconciliation register records changed emphasis and unresolved conflicts. Familiarity does not consume skillcap and is distinct from broader discipline/affinity Mastery. Under the Proposed no-ordinary-120 direction, Magery 100 is technical mastery, not magical completion: rare patterns, schools, modifiers, practice, preparation, grimoires, rituals, regional knowledge, rare reagents, player execution and Inscription sustain long-term progression. No ordinary skill cap or spell availability is changed.
