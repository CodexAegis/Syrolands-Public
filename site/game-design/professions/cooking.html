Cooking ​

Design status: Proposed; all mechanics, examples and technique paths below remain candidates. Implementation: no implementation of this redesign is established by this documentation batch. Availability: not established. Verification: documentation review only; no gameplay test. Reviewed: 2026-10-09 for public projection; design status and mechanics preserved from the canonical specification.

Player-facing overview · Profession System

Purpose and profession loop ​

Cooking should be a physical world activity, not a recipe-list menu.

Stations ​

  • wilderness cooking stand built around a campfire (via Camping);
  • kitchen/stove/oven/grill in houses, inns, taverns;
  • possible richer camp setups at higher Camping.

Station interface ​

  • represents the station;
  • drag/drop ingredients from backpack;
  • areas such as pot, grill, preparation table;
  • ingredient starts cooking immediately when placed.

Ingredients have cooking states/windows, e.g.:

  • raw;
  • rare;
  • medium;
  • well done;
  • burnt;

or equivalent ingredient-specific states.

Different ingredients have different timing/state progressions.

Skill should widen tolerances and improve control rather than automate all skill.

Possible thermal abstraction:

  • Low
  • Medium
  • High
  • Very High

Fuel/fire quality can connect with Camping/Lumberjacking without requiring excessive simulation.

Recipes ​

  • can require multiple ingredients at specific preparation states;
  • final assembly happens at a preparation/assembly area;
  • recipes can be discovered via exploration, books, cooks, cultures, quests, ruins, experimentation.

Recipe quality may depend on:

  • ingredient quality;
  • cooking state/timing;
  • skill;
  • station;
  • execution.

Food should not merely become potion-like stat buffs. Prefer effects around:

  • satiation;
  • nutrition;
  • recovery;
  • stamina efficiency;
  • fatigue;
  • temperature resistance;
  • concentration/recovery;
  • situational survival benefits.

Taste Codex / personal taste ​

  • each character can discover personal food preferences through eating;
  • preferences should not be shown fully at character creation;
  • discovering a favorite dish/family can produce especially high satisfaction/comfort effects;
  • this gives players an incentive to try new foods;
  • chefs benefit from broad culinary knowledge and completion of their culinary journal.

This can become a profession endgame.

Knowledge and specialization ​

Culinary Journal / Taste Codex. Cooking styles, recipes and regional food knowledge; a broad journal and mastery of preparation can sustain a culinary endgame.

Shared progression, production and economy ​

The character skill framework owns teaching and cap/gain direction. The Profession System owns craft discovery, knowledge sharing, manual mastery, batch production, commissions and multidimensional reputation. Those rules are not independently redefined here. The quality specification owns the shared result envelope and rare item properties.

Related skills and field interactions ​

Camping supplies field fire/stations; Lumberjacking informs fuel; food recovery may intersect Healing and the proposed Meditation/Concentration system. Exact effects and stacking are unresolved.

Alchemy · Lumberjacking · Camping and world knowledge · Meditation and Concentration.

Abstraction and technical boundary ​

Keep only state and feedback that support meaningful decisions. The shared abstraction/authority constraints and living-world architecture apply; persistence requires a separate technical design. Nothing here selects packet IDs, schemas, timers, UI assets or runtime algorithms.

Open questions ​

Cooking windows and feedback precision; station access and ingredient ownership; recipe/assembly failure; fuel/heat abstraction; taste discovery, persistence and satisfaction limits; recovery versus medicine and potion stacking. No nutrition formula or free combat buff is established.

The shared reconciliation register owns cross-system conflicts. Existing combat experiments and evidence do not validate this profession redesign.
