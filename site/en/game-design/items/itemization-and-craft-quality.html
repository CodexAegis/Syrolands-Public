Itemization & Craft Quality ​

Design status: Proposed; tiers, ranges, dimensions, traits and future sets are not finalized balance. Implementation: this redesign is documentation only; existing legacy items are not proof of these mechanics. Availability: not established for the proposed system. Verification: documentation review, no runtime validation. Reviewed: 2026-10-09 for public projection; design status and mechanics preserved from the canonical specification.

Player-facing overview · Profession System · Equipment and loot

Scope and design rationale ​

One compatible language should describe crafted items and PvM drops, including high-end quality, traits and resonance. Crafting must remain a valid endgame equipment source rather than becoming automatically obsolete beside monster loot. Quality should express how well an object performs its intended role, not an unlimited damage ladder.

This page owns cross-profession item quality. The Profession System owns progression, knowledge, manual/batch production and commissions; profession pages own processes and materials. Existing accepted horizontal equipment, meaningful loss and combat authority remain unchanged.

What determines quality ​

Skill determines ceiling. Techniques determine what can be attempted. Materials determine potential. Execution determines where the result lands. Small RNG creates exceptional variation.

Chance is secondary to mastery, materials and process. Bad materials and novice execution must never produce a legendary item purely through luck. An Exceptional result should emerge from the process/material/skill combination rather than a simple exceptional-quality roll. Batch consistency follows the shared production constraints, not a separate random failure model.

Weapon quality dimensions ​

The first-pass model deliberately uses about five conceptual dimensions. They are not five approved serialized floats or exact UI stats.

DimensionIntended meaning
Offensive EfficiencyHow well a correctly executed attack converts into its intended injury/effect
HandlingStamina, recovery and control characteristics
Precision / ConsistencyVariability, repeatability and support for precise or targeted attacks
IntegrityResistance to deformation, breakage and structural failure
Functional RetentionHow long intended performance survives use; edge retention for edged weapons, appropriate equivalents for other families

Weapon family defines behavior; quality determines how well it performs that behavior. A Common war hammer should still cause more blunt trauma than an Exceptional dagger. Spear quality concerns penetration, alignment and stability; swords concern cutting/thrust efficiency and edge retention; bows concern projectile energy, handling and consistency. These are design examples rather than established damage comparisons or checkout measurements.

Item structural Integrity is not body-region integrity and does not inherit its numerical scale. Quality Precision does not guarantee an aimed hit or change server authority.

Common versus Exceptional in play ​

A better weapon may offer modestly better offensive efficiency, better crossing of armor thresholds, handling/recovery, consistency, durability/integrity, functional retention and lower degradation during long encounters or expeditions. It must not simply mean more damage or “Exceptional = +20% DPS.”

Common longswordExceptional longsword
Perfectly usable baselineModestly better cutting/thrust behavior
Reasonable handlingBetter balance and consistency
Reasonable durabilityBetter structural integrity and longer functional edge retention

The source motivates small differences through thresholds, localized trauma, penetration, armor interaction and equipment wear. Their full proposed integration is not established by the current combat slice. The accepted experimental Local Trauma policy and existing armor adapter remain authoritative for that slice. No new coefficients, threshold model or attack timing is approved here.

Masterworks ​

A Masterwork is rare and prestigious even for a GM/master crafter. It represents extraordinary convergence of skill, advanced/master techniques, exceptional materials, excellent process/execution and favorable small variation. Skill 100 alone should not routinely create many Masterworks. Masterwork does not automatically mean magical, nor does it guarantee a special trait.

Special traits and resonances ​

High-end items may rarely acquire properties that change play. Prefer situational sidegrades, build interactions and gameplay modifiers over massive flat damage bonuses. Candidate examples include:

  • Repeated hits building Frost on a limb.
  • Greater structural damage to shields.
  • Better interaction with already-traumatized regions.
  • Temporary concentration disruption.
  • Specialized hunting interactions.

These examples are Proposed, not a live effect catalogue. Traits should have provenance, material/process justification, compatibility requirements and balance tradeoffs/counterplay. A random iron dagger should not gain freezing powers without reason. The magic proposal, local trauma and creature anatomy need explicit coordination before any effect is implemented.

Latent properties and item history ​

Some high-end crafted or dropped items may contain inactive resonance. Specific use, defeating a particular creature, exposure to a place/environment, a ritual, long-term use or another exceptional condition could awaken it. Use this sparingly so discovery remains special.

A Frostwood bow awakening cold resonance after a significant cold-related event illustrates the idea; it does not establish a material catalogue, quest, awakening threshold or guaranteed cold trait. Item history should create memorable objects without requiring every high-end item to carry latent magic. Bowmaking owns the craft path, not resonance activation rules.

Future sacred or resonant sets ​

Future / Proposed. Two or more individually exceptional compatible items with related properties might be ritually bound or consecrated into a Sacred/Resonant Set by a high-end specialist. A priest/ritualist is an example contingent on future Chivalry, religion or Inscription design, not an adopted class or system.

Sets should create interesting synergy rather than generic large stat stacks. They may be costly, rare and possibly irreversible; exact costs and reversibility are unresolved. Preserve provenance/history and avoid mandatory Best-in-Slot power creep.

This is distinct from the unapproved Sacred Item retention idea in equipment and loot. The similar name does not grant death protection, insurance or an exception to meaningful loss.

Sidegrades, economy and anti-abuse constraints ​

Exceptional properties should broaden or specialize gameplay more often than increase raw power. An ordinary excellent item remains competitive; special items contribute tactical options, specialization, identity and prestige rather than automatic dominance.

Commissions should value fitness for purpose, workmanship and consistency, not merely the highest tier label. No prices, drop rates, Masterwork probability, salvage loop or reputation weights are selected. Existing meaningful loss must coexist with prestigious histories without silently adding protection. Trait combinations, batch production, awakening conditions and long-term attribution need abuse review in a later design pass.

Abstraction and technical boundary ​

Use the shared compact-state constraints. Material/process provenance, retained performance, traits and awakening history must earn their state cost through gameplay value. Do not infer a persistent float for every conceptual dimension or a tick for every inactive property.

Persistence/lifecycle, server authority and living-world architecture supply constraints, not a ready-made item schema. Save compatibility, migrations, disclosure and legacy item mapping require a separately authorized technical design.

Open questions and reconciliation ​

  • Final tier names, envelopes, ceiling interactions and small-RNG bounds; no hard skill unlocks yet.
  • How non-weapon items express quality without blindly applying the five weapon dimensions.
  • Weapon-family and armor thresholds, wear/repair and functional-retention curves; no changes to the experimental residual ×2 trauma rule.
  • Mapping existing legacy qualities/materials/items to this language without silently rewriting their semantics.
  • Learning advanced techniques, measuring manual execution and preserving batch consistency.
  • Trait compatibility, counterplay, stacking and interpretation by future pain, bleeding or Concentration systems.
  • Discovery/disclosure of latent properties, eligible awakening events and repeated-event abuse.
  • Crafted/drop economic parity, scarcity and ordinary-item competitiveness.
  • Sacred/resonant set specialist, requirements, cost, reversibility and naming versus Sacred Item retention; no loss exception approved.

See the cross-system reconciliation register. Existing decisions, localized combat experiment and slice evidence retain their scope and do not validate this proposed item system.
