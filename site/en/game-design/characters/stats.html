Stats and combat responsiveness ​

Design status: Proposed; open questions remain Unresolved. Referenced Accepted decisions retain their existing scope. Implementation/availability: no changes to gameplay or runtime configuration; this redesign is not established as live. Verification: documentation review, not a playtest; current availability is not established here. Reviewed: 2026-10-09 for public projection; design status and mechanics preserved from the canonical specification.

Player-facing overview · Character progression

Responsiveness is baseline ​

Stats modify capability; they should not make basic control feel like slow motion. Low stats must not make an early character feel artificially broken or unusably slow. A new low-DEX character should attack at a believable normal cadence. This is a Proposed experience requirement, not proof that inherited timing has already changed.

DEX: bounded control and recovery ​

DEX should not dominate basic swing/animation speed so strongly that low-DEX characters become unusable. Base cadence should primarily reflect weapon class, stance, attack type, recovery, stamina/fatigue, encumbrance and technique. These influences are candidates, not a completed formula or proof that stances/fatigue systems exist.

Within bounded margins, DEX may influence recovery, aim correction, defensive responsiveness, balance, chained actions and precision under pressure. Magnitudes and stacking are unresolved.

STR: handling and physical capability ​

STR should not mean every weapon deals proportionally more damage. Potential roles include heavy-weapon and heavy-armor handling, stamina cost/endurance relationships, grappling/physical contests, control of high-draw bows, reasonable blunt-force potential and load/encumbrance. Weapon design and technique remain primary determinants of weapon behavior. No grappling system, draw-weight formula or armor requirement is finalized.

INT: broader mental capability ​

INT should be reconsidered beyond mana pool size, in the context of Magery, Meditation, Concentration, spell knowledge and mental states. Possible influences include cognitive capacity, mental resilience, concentration-related behavior and magical resource efficiency/capacity. This is not a complete INT system, new resource or finalized formula.

Stats support skill identity ​

ExampleIntended separation
Heavy war hammerSTR helps handling; Macing, Tactics and weapon design express expertise
ShootingDEX supports recovery/control; Archery and player execution express shooting skill
MagicINT supports mental capability; Magery, Meditation, Concentration and knowledge express magical expertise

Avoid universal dominant stat multipliers that flatten builds. These examples preserve skill roles without deciding future mandatory skill combinations.

Cadence, attack opportunities and animation ​

Future combat/technical work must distinguish:

  • Animation responsiveness: visual feedback and presentation timing.
  • Attack recovery: time and constraints before another action/opportunity.
  • Actual attack opportunities: authoritative occasions to resolve an attack.
  • Stamina/fatigue constraints: resource-related limitations, with formulas open.
  • Weapon speed: family/weapon contribution, not automatically an animation playback rate.

Faster-looking animation does not grant more attacks. Server timing must never wait for client animation completion, and ordinary combat remains automatic under the existing combat foundations.

BoundaryExample
USER INTENTRequest an attack or technique
AUTHORITATIVE SERVER STATEValidate opportunity, resources, recovery and outcome
SYNCHRONIZED STATEApproved action/outcome and timing information
CLIENT PRESENTATIONResponsive input feedback and coherent animation

No renderer, animation assets, network contracts or combat timers are changed here.

Reconciliation and open questions ​

Inherited pre-AOS timing depends on current stamina and weapon speed, with DEX influencing stamina capacity. Calling it simply a raw-DEX swing formula would be inaccurate. The new direction is an experience requirement and an implementation gap; exact changes need separate combat design and gameplay measurement.

Inherited STR damage scaling, INT/mana coupling, total stat cap, configured per-stat maximum/gain and creation values are not selected Syrolands redesign values. Existing source defaults may be overridden; no runtime configuration was inspected.

Unresolved: total stat cap and stat ranges; stat gain and locks; exact DEX recovery/control contribution; STR weapon/armor and stamina relationships; INT/mana/Concentration model; fatigue/encumbrance effects; weapon cadence ranges; separation of visual timing and authoritative opportunity; skill-specific exceptions; legacy-character conversion. Skill progression and creation must be reconciled before implementation. No new stat formula or balance is established.

Active Memory — Batch 04 ​

The latest memory proposal emphasizes INT as cognitive workspace for Active Memory capacity, simultaneous pattern complexity and advanced variants. Simple patterns may cost less memory than heavily modified ones. This strengthens the Proposed direction above without resolving INT versus mana, stat ranges, slots or capacity formulas. Meditation organization and per-pattern Familiarity are distinct contributions, not additional INT multipliers already selected.

Martial preparation — Batch 05 ​

Prepared Combat Repertoire tests five slots only as a UX starting point. Do not hard-bind them to INT. Small extra capacity, faster changes or complex-technique support are later candidates, not a mandatory warrior tax. The magic cognitive-workspace proposal retains its separate unresolved capacity model.

Core stats and stamina capacity — Batch 08 ​

Proposed direction; Max Stamina remains Unresolved. Core stats remain STR, DEX and INT. Athletics is a skill/proposed physical specialization, not a fourth core stat. No CON stat is introduced.

ContributionProposed boundary
STRForce, heavy equipment handling and load; may support exertion capacity, but is not selected as the sole stamina stat
DEXResponsiveness, recovery/control and precision under pressure; low DEX must not mean slow-motion play
INTCognitive workspace, magical Active Memory and concentration-related capacity; not a mandatory martial stat merely to obtain SA slots
AthleticsCandidate stamina efficiency, recovery, sustained exertion, sprinting and lunging; exact effects and numbers remain open

No Max Stamina formula based on STR, DEX, Athletics or a new CON stat is finalized. The Experimental combat test's fixed 100 stamina pool is a test fixture, not that formula.

A Proposed evaluation hypothesis, not Accepted design, is to derive Endurance/Stamina Capacity from core stats plus Athletics, conditions and encumbrance while retaining the three core stats. Weights, caps, interactions and whether to adopt that derived value remain Unresolved.
