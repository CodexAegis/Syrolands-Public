Weapons and damage ​

Status: Proposed · PROTOTYPE numeric reference · Not implemented.

TL;DR ​

  • Choose a weapon for its spacing, tempo and commitment, not only its damage.
  • Cutting, piercing and blunt impact describe different intended threats.
  • Quality improves a weapon within its role; it does not remove its trade-offs.

How it works ​

AspectMeaning
SwordVersatile cuts and thrusts; reliable close control with limited reach.
SpearSpacing and piercing pressure; harder to use when the enemy gets inside its preferred range.
War hammerCommitted blunt impact; a miss leaves costly recovery.
Dagger / axePrecise short openings / committed cutting and structural impact.

Bleeding, fractures and armor penetration are future possibilities, not automatic effects of a damage family.

Rules reference: PROTOTYPE = numeric test, not implemented or final balance. PROPOSED = design direction. UNRESOLVED / TBD = open value. LIVE requires current playable verification; EXPERIMENTAL requires an implemented test slice. Design acceptance and availability are separate.

Practiced, neutral handling, Stable. Basics ignore Familiarity; learned SAs require preparation. Guard Break halves active frontal stance reduction: Brace 35% → 17.5%, Riposte 25% → 12.5%; it triggers neither counter. No lingering debuff, armor bypass or durability damage. Sweep and Crushing Arc select at most 2 hostile targets at first active contact; selected target first, then nearest, stable identity tie-break; no allies or incidental neutrals.

Compared with classic UO ​

Classic UOSyrolands
Weapon families already have distinct speeds and strengthsMake action choice, reach and recoverable commitment central to that identity.
Maces already interact with armor and stamina in UORExpand physical interactions without treating every blunt hit as a guaranteed fracture.

Example ​

A missed Heavy Blow gives a swordsman time to respond. Greater impact is useful only if you can create the opening and afford the recovery.
